Paste a YouTube video of an open–close–open drum roll (slow–fast–slow, also called slow to fast). The app finds the tempo ramp and plots only that part. Each bar is one stroke: its width spans until the next stroke, its height is that duration in milliseconds, or the tempo in strokes per minute.

Scroll to zoom · drag to pan · double-click to reset · click a bar to seek · green bars are inferred strokes
