Iskumetri

Choose an audio file of an open–close–open drum roll (slow–fast–slow, also called slow to fast). Your audio stays in this browser. The app finds the tempo ramp and plots only that part. Each bar is one stroke: its width spans until the next stroke, its height is that duration in milliseconds, or the tempo in strokes per minute.

Up to 20 minutes and 128 MiB. WAV and MP3 recommended. The analyzer loads on first use.

YouTube audio extraction

YouTube does not let other websites read its player data or audio streams, so this static app cannot extract them. Choose a local audio file above to analyze it. No audio is sent to a server.

Scroll to zoom · drag to pan · double-click to reset · click a bar to seek · green outlines mark inferred strokes

Relative volume: quiet loud. More solid bars indicate louder strokes.
